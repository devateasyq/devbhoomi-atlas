National Parks, Sanctuaries and Wildlife

Five national parks

  • Great Himalayan National Park (Kullu) — notified 1984, declared 1999; UNESCO World Heritage Site, 2014.
  • Pin Valley National Park (Lahaul & Spiti), 1987 — snow leopard and Siberian ibex; a cold-desert park.
  • Inderkilla National Park (Kullu), 2010.
  • Khirganga National Park (Kullu), 2010.
  • Simbalbara (Col. Sher Jung) National Park (Sirmaur), 2010.

Sanctuaries

Around 26–28 wildlife sanctuaries and 3 conservation reserves. The ones asked by name: Pong Dam Lake (Kangra, birds), Renuka (Sirmaur), Kalatop–Khajjiar (Chamba), Kais and Kanawar (Kullu), Chail (Solan), Daranghati and Majathal (Shimla), Rupi Bhaba (Kinnaur), Kibber (Spiti, the largest by area).

Disputed The sanctuary count differs between sources (26, 28 and 30 all appear) because of reclassifications. State the number as 'about 28' or quote the latest HP Forest Department figure.

State symbols

  • State bird — Western Tragopan (jujurana, 'king of birds').
  • State animal — Snow leopard.
  • State tree — Deodar (Cedrus deodara).
  • State flower — Pink rhododendron (buransh).
  • State fish — Golden mahseer.

Forests

  • Recorded forest area about 37,033 km² — roughly 66.5% of the geographical area, but much of it is above the tree line.
  • Actual forest cover is about 15,400 km², around 27–28% of the state (Forest Survey of India). The gap between the two figures is itself an exam point.
  • Types by altitude: tropical dry deciduous → chir pine → ban oak / deodar / kail → fir and spruce → alpine pasture (bugyal / thach).
  • Deodar, chir pine, kail, fir, spruce, oak and rhododendron are the species to name.
