Hydropower — 'Urja Pradesh'

Himachal is called Urja Pradesh. Hydel is the state's largest own-source revenue and its biggest single infrastructure story.

  • Identified potential of about 27,436 MW across five basins — Sutlej, Beas, Ravi, Chenab and Yamuna. The Sutlej basin holds the largest share.
  • Roughly 11,000 MW has been harnessed; the state receives 12% free power (rising with project age) as royalty, plus 1% for local area development.
  • Agencies: BBMB, NHPC, SJVN, NTPC, HPPCL, HPSEBL and private developers.

Major projects to know

  • Bhakra — 1,325 MW (Sutlej, Bilaspur) — 226 m high, completed 1963; Gobind Sagar.
  • Nathpa Jhakri — 1,500 MW (Sutlej, Kinnaur/Shimla), SJVN, commissioned 2004 — the largest underground hydel station in India.
  • Karcham Wangtoo — 1,045 MW (Sutlej, Kinnaur), JSW.
  • Kol Dam — 800 MW (Sutlej, Bilaspur/Mandi), NTPC.
  • Dehar / Beas–Sutlej Link — 990 MW (Mandi) — diverts Beas water to the Sutlej through the Pandoh–Baggi tunnel.
  • Parbati II and III (Beas basin, Kullu), NHPC.
  • Chamera I, II, III (Ravi, Chamba), NHPC; Baira Siul — 180 MW, NHPC's first commissioned project in India.
  • Pong — 396 MW (Beas, Kangra); Larji — 126 MW (Beas, Kullu); Giri — 60 MW (Sirmaur).
  • Shanan — 110 MW (Uhl, Jogindernagar, Mandi), commissioned 1932 — the oldest project in the state, built under Raja Joginder Sen; its lease to Punjab is a live inter-state dispute.

Costs and controversy

  • Run-of-the-river schemes dewater long river stretches; the Sutlej in Kinnaur is the standard example.
  • Muck disposal, tunnelling-induced slope failure, and drying of springs; the Kinnaur anti-dam movement.
  • GLOF and flash-flood risk to project structures — the Parechu event of 2005.
Exam hook 27,436 MW potential; five basins; Sutlej the largest. Nathpa Jhakri 1,500 MW; Bhakra 1,325 MW; Shanan 1932 (oldest).
