Topic

Fairs and Festivals

International and state-level fairs

  • Kullu Dussehra — begins on Vijaya Dashami, when Dussehra ends elsewhere, and runs seven days; the deities of the valley assemble before Raghunathji. Traced to Raja Jagat Singh, c. 1651. International status.
  • Mandi Shivratri — the deities of the district assemble before Madho Rai; international.
  • Minjar fair (Chamba) — July/August; a minjar (maize silk) is offered to the Ravi. State level.
  • Lavi fair (Rampur Bushahr) — November; the great trans-Himalayan trade fair, traced to Kehri Singh's treaty with Tibet.
  • Renukaji fair (Sirmaur) — November, at the largest natural lake; the meeting of Renuka and Parshuram.
  • Shoolini fair (Solan) — June. Nalwari (Bilaspur) — a cattle fair begun in 1889.
  • Sui Mata (Chamba) — commemorating Champavati.
  • Manimahesh yatra (Chamba), Naina Devi, Chintpurni and Jwalamukhi Navratra fairs.

Tribal and seasonal festivals

  • Losar — the Tibetan new year, in Lahaul, Spiti and Kinnaur.
  • Halda — Lahaul's fire festival, resembling Diwali.
  • Fulaich / Phulaich — the flower festival of Kinnaur, in memory of the dead.
  • Ladarcha — the old Spiti trade fair at Kaza.
  • Phagli — a mask festival of Kullu and Lahaul; Sair, Saazo, Bishu, Beeshu.
Exam hook International: Kullu Dussehra and Mandi Shivratri. Trade: Lavi (Rampur). Flower: Fulaich (Kinnaur). Fire: Halda (Lahaul).
