Topic

Lakes and Wetlands

Natural lakes

  • Renuka (Sirmaur) — the largest natural lake in Himachal Pradesh; shaped like a reclining woman; the Renukaji fair is held here.
  • Rewalsar / Tso Pema (Mandi) — sacred to Hindus, Buddhists and Sikhs; associated with Padmasambhava.
  • Prashar (Mandi) — 2,730 m, with a three-tiered pagoda temple and a floating island.
  • Kamrunag (Mandi), Sareolsar (Kullu, near Jalori).
  • Chandra Tal (Lahaul) — about 4,300 m, source of the Chandra; a Ramsar site (2005).
  • Suraj Tal (Lahaul) — near Baralacha, one of the highest lakes in India; source of the Bhaga.
  • Bhrigu and Dashair (Kullu).
  • Manimahesh (Chamba) — the Manimahesh yatra; also Lama Dal, Khajjiar, Ghadasaru, Mahakali.
  • Nako (Kinnaur).

Man-made reservoirs

  • Gobind Sagar (Bhakra, Sutlej, Bilaspur) — the largest man-made lake in the state.
  • Maharana Pratap Sagar / Pong (Beas, Kangra) — a Ramsar site (2002) and a major bird sanctuary.
  • Chamera (Ravi, Chamba), Pandoh (Beas, Mandi), Kol Dam (Sutlej).

Ramsar sites — all three

  • Pong Dam Lake — designated 2002.
  • Renuka — designated 2005.
  • Chandra Taal — designated 2005.
Exam hook Largest natural lake: Renuka. Largest artificial: Gobind Sagar. Three Ramsar sites: Pong (2002), Renuka (2005), Chandra Taal (2005).
