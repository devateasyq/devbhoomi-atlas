Topic

Mountain Passes

Give the height, the range, and what it connects — that is what the question asks.

  • Rohtang La — 3,978 m (Pir Panjal) — Kullu to Lahaul. Bypassed since 2020 by the Atal Tunnel, 9.02 km, opened 3 October 2020.
  • Kunzum La — 4,551 m — Lahaul (Chandra valley) to Spiti.
  • Baralacha La — 4,890 m — Lahaul to Ladakh; the Chandra and Bhaga both rise near it, as does Suraj Tal.
  • Shipki La — 3,930 m (Kinnaur) — to Tibet; the Sutlej enters India here. A designated border-trade point.
  • Sach Pass — 4,420 m — Chamba to Pangi.
  • Jalori Pass — 3,120 m — Kullu (Outer Seraj) to Shimla.
  • Hamta — 4,270 m — Kullu to Lahaul.
  • Chanshal — 4,520 m — Rohru to Dodra Kwar, Shimla; the highest motorable pass in Shimla district.
  • Kugti — 5,040 m — Bharmour (Chamba) to Lahaul; the traditional Gaddi migration route.
  • Pin Parvati — 5,319 m — Kullu (Parvati valley) to Spiti.
  • Parang La — 5,578 m — Spiti to Ladakh (Tso Moriri).
  • Shinku/Shingo La — 5,090 m — Lahaul to Zanskar.
  • Indrahar, Waru, Thamsar, Baleni — Dhauladhar crossings from Kangra to Chamba/Bara Bangahal.
  • Drati, Kalicho, Chobia — Chamba to Lahaul.
  • Bhubu Jot, Dulchi, Bashleo — Mandi/Kullu internal passes.
  • Padri — Chamba to Jammu.
Exam hook Highest in the list: Parang La (5,578 m). The Sutlej enters at Shipki La. Chandra and Bhaga both rise at Baralacha. Kugti is the Gaddi route.
