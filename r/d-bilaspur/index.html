Formed from the princely state of Kahlur, Bilaspur was a separate Part-C state from 1948 until it was merged into Himachal Pradesh on 1 July 1954 as its fifth district — the only district to join by merger of a full state rather than by transfer or reorganisation.

The old town of Bilaspur was submerged by the Gobind Sagar reservoir of the Bhakra dam; a new town was laid out on higher ground. Bilaspur is the first fully planned town of independent India in this sense, and the displacement it caused is a standing question on rehabilitation policy.

  • Naina Devi — one of the Shakti Peeths, on a hilltop overlooking Gobind Sagar.
  • Bhakra Dam (on the Sutlej, at Bhakra village) — foundation 1948, commissioned 1963.
  • Kandror bridge over the Sutlej, once Asia's highest arch bridge.
  • Nalwari fair — the cattle fair started in 1889 by W. Goldstein.
Exam hook Bilaspur is the answer to three different questions: 5th district (1 July 1954), the Kahlur state (founded 697 CE by Bir Chand), and the Bhakra displacement. Keep the three separate.
