Event

Himachal becomes a Part C state

On the commencement of the Constitution, Himachal Pradesh became a Part C state under a Lieutenant Governor. A Legislative Assembly of 36 members was provided under the Government of Part C States Act, 1951.

The first election was held in 1952; the Congress won 24 seats and Dr. Y. S. Parmar became the first Chief Minister on 8 March 1952.
