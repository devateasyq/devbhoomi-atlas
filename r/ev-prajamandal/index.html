The Praja Mandals are formed

The All India States People's Conference (1927) gave the movement in the princely states a national frame; in the hills it took the form of Praja Mandals — people's associations demanding civil rights, an end to begar, and responsible government under the raja.

  • Chamba — People's Defence League / Servants of the People, 1932 and 1936.
  • Mandi — Praja Mandal, 1936.
  • Baghat — 1938.
  • Himalayan Riyasati Praja Mandal, Shimla — the umbrella body, with Bhagmal Sautha as president and Pandit Padam Dev as secretary.
  • Kunihar — 1939; Dhami — 13 July 1939; Sirmaur — 1939 and again 1944.
  • Bilaspur — 1945, under Daulat Ram Sankhyan and Narotam Dutt Shastri.
Disputed Founding years for several Praja Mandals differ between sources (the Himalayan Riyasati Praja Mandal is dated both 1938 and 1939). Where a year is contested, give the movement and the leader — those are stable.
