Panchayati Raj and Local Government

Himachal was an early and enthusiastic adopter of panchayati raj, and the state's own Act predates the 73rd Amendment.

  • Three tiers under the HP Panchayati Raj Act, 1994: Gram Panchayat → Panchayat Samiti (block) → Zila Parishad (district).
  • About 3,615 gram panchayats, 78 development blocks and 12 zila parishads (figures change with delimitation — check the latest Economic Survey).
  • 50% reservation for women in panchayati raj institutions and urban local bodies — Himachal was among the first states to raise it from one-third.
  • Gram Sabha meetings are mandatory; HP has used them for social audit of MGNREGA.
  • Urban bodies: Municipal Corporations (Shimla, Dharamshala, Solan, Mandi, Palampur and others — the number has grown; verify current count), Municipal Councils and Nagar Panchayats.
  • Shimla Municipal Corporation is among the oldest municipal bodies in India, dating to 1851.
Exam hook HP Panchayati Raj Act 1994; three tiers; 50% women's reservation. The 73rd and 74th Amendments (1992, effective 1993) are the constitutional frame.
