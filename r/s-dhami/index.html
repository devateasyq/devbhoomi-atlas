Dhami

Seatdhami
CapitalHalog (Dhami)
DynastyRana
GroupSimla Hill States
FounderAn offshoot of the Kahlur house (tradition)
Foundedc. 1815 as a separate sanad state
Merged15 April 1948

A very small state near Shimla, remembered for one day: 16 July 1939, when the Rana's men fired on a Praja Mandal crowd at Halog, killing Durga Das (Uma Dutt by some accounts) — the first firing on a popular movement in the Himachal hills.

The immediate cause was the ban on the Dhami Prem Prachani Sabha and the arrest of Bhagmal Sautha, who was leading a procession to present demands to the Rana.
