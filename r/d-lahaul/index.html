Lahaul and Spiti

The largest district by area (13,835 km², about a quarter of the state) and the smallest by population (31,564), with a density of 2 persons per km² — the lowest in Himachal and among the lowest in India. It was also the only district to record a negative decadal growth rate in 2011.

Two distinct valleys under one administration: Lahaul (Chandra and Bhaga, meeting at Tandi to form the Chenab) and Spiti (the Spiti river, a Sutlej tributary), separated by the Kunzum pass. Both are cold deserts in the rain shadow of the Great Himalayan range and are Scheduled Tribe areas.

  • Tabo monastery (996 CE) — the 'Ajanta of the Himalayas', founded by Rinchen Zangpo; a Monument of National Importance.
  • Key, Dhankar, Kardang, Shashur, Guru Ghantal, Tayul monasteries.
  • Baralacha La, Kunzum La, Rohtang, Shinku La, Parang La passes; the Atal Tunnel (opened 3 October 2020, 9.02 km) under Rohtang.
  • Chandra Tal and Suraj Tal (among the highest lakes in India).
  • Pin Valley National Park — snow leopard and Siberian ibex habitat.
Exam hook Largest area, smallest population, lowest density, negative growth, cold desert, Tabo 996 CE, Chandra + Bhaga = Chenab at Tandi. Almost every 'extreme value' question about HP points here.
