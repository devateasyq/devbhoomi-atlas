Topic

Languages, Literature and Folk Arts

Languages

  • Official: Hindi; second official: Sanskrit (16 February 2019).
  • The spoken languages are Western Pahari dialects, often mutually unintelligible: Kangri, Mandeali, Kulvi, Chambeali, Bilaspuri (Kahluri), Mahasui, Sirmauri, Bhattiyali, Gaddi, Bharmauri, Pangwali, Kinnauri, Lahauli (Bhoti).
  • Kinnauri, Lahauli and Spitian belong to the Tibeto-Burman family, unlike the rest, which are Indo-Aryan.
  • Historic scripts: Tankri (Takri) — the script of the hill states' administration and the copper plates; Sharda in Chamba's early inscriptions; Bhoti in the Buddhist valleys. All now written in Devanagari.

Folk dance

  • Nati — the signature circle dance; Kullu Nati entered the Guinness records in 2015 as the largest folk dance.
  • Kayang, Bakayang, Bnayangchu, Jataru Kayang — Kinnaur.
  • Shand and Shabu, Namgen — Lahaul; Chham — masked monastic dance.
  • Burah, Thoda — Sirmaur and Shimla; Thoda is an archery contest-dance between Pandava and Kaurava 'sides'.
  • Dangi, Gaddi dance, Dandras, Dharveshi — Chamba and the Gaddi belt; Jhoori, Jhanjhar, Gee, Luddi, Rakshasa.

Literature and figures

  • Kalhana's Rajatarangini for early references; the vamshavalis of each state.
  • Yashpal — Hindi novelist and HSRA revolutionary.
  • Baba Kanshi Ram — Pahari poet of the freedom movement.
  • Nicholas Roerich — the Russian painter who settled at Naggar (Kullu); his estate is now a museum.
  • Rudyard Kipling — Shimla in Kim and the Plain Tales from the Hills.
