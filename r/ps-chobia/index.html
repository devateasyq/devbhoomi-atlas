Chobia Pass

Chobia Pass, 4,966 m in the Pir Panjal, is one of a group of three passes out of Chamba into Lahaul — Drati, Kalicho and Chobia — that are usually listed together rather than asked about individually.

Exam hook 4,966 m · Pir Panjal · Chamba to Lahaul · one of the Drati–Kalicho–Chobia group.
