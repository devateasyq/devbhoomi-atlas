Topic

Constitutional Evolution of Himachal Pradesh

The single most reliably asked chronology in the HP papers. Learn it as a ladder of eight steps.

  • 15 April 1948 — Chief Commissioner's Province, by integration of about thirty hill states. Four districts: Chamba, Mandi, Mahasu, Sirmaur. First Chief Commissioner: N. C. Mehta.
  • 26 January 1950 — becomes a Part C state under a Lieutenant Governor; a 36-member Assembly is provided in 1951.
  • 1952 — first election; Dr. Y. S. Parmar becomes the first Chief Minister on 8 March 1952.
  • 1 July 1954 — Bilaspur merged as the fifth district.
  • 1 November 1956 — States Reorganisation Act: becomes a Union Territory; Assembly dissolved, Territorial Council created.
  • 1 May 1960 — Kinnaur created as the sixth district.
  • 1 July 1963 — legislature restored under the Government of Union Territories Act, 1963; Parmar returns as Chief Minister.
  • 1 November 1966 — Punjab Reorganisation Act: Kangra, Kullu, Lahaul & Spiti, Shimla, Nalagarh, parts of Una, and Dalhousie–Bakloh transferred. Ten districts.
  • 18 December 1970 — the State of Himachal Pradesh Act passed by Parliament (assent 31 December 1970).
  • 25 January 1971 — Himachal becomes the 18th state; proclaimed by Indira Gandhi at the Ridge. The High Court is established the same day.
  • 1 September 1972 — reorganisation into twelve districts: Una, Hamirpur and Solan created; Mahasu dissolved into Shimla.
Exam hook Three dates carry the most marks: 15 April 1948 (formation), 1 November 1966 (Punjab transfer), 25 January 1971 (statehood). The trap is Bilaspur — 1954, not 1948.
Disputed The number of states integrated in 1948 is given as 26, 28, 30 or 31 depending on whether feudatories and thakurais are counted. 30 is the usual answer; say 'about thirty' if you must justify it.
