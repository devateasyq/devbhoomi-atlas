Pin Glacier

FeedsPin, which joins the Spiti, which joins the Sutlej at Khab

Pin Glacier lies in the Pin valley of Spiti, inside the Pin Valley National Park, and feeds the Pin river — which joins the Spiti, and the Spiti in turn joins the Sutlej at Khab.

The Pin valley is cold-desert country, one of the driest tracts in the state, and its glacier sits squarely in the Chandra and Spiti belt that this roster's standard retreat question is built around — recession here is watched for its bearing on the Sutlej's hydel schemes downstream and on glacial-lake outburst flood risk.
