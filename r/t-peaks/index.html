Topic

Peaks and Glaciers

Peaks

  • Reo Purgyil (Leo Pargial) — 6,816 m, Kinnaur — the highest point in Himachal Pradesh.
  • Shigri Parbat — 6,526 m, Lahaul.
  • Dharmsura (White Sail) — 6,446 m, Lahaul/Kullu.
  • Indrasan — 6,221 m, Kullu.
  • Shilla — 6,132 m, Kinnaur/Spiti.
  • Kinner Kailash — 6,050 m, Kinnaur — the 79-foot rock Shivling.
  • Deo Tibba — 6,001 m, Kullu.
  • Hanuman Tibba — 5,928 m, Dhauladhar.
  • Gepang Goh — 5,870 m, Lahaul.
  • Manimahesh Kailash — 5,653 m, Chamba.
  • Churdhar — 3,647 m, Sirmaur — the highest peak of the outer Himalaya in HP.

Glaciers

  • Bara Shigri — the largest glacier in Himachal Pradesh, in the Chandra valley, Lahaul.
  • Chandra Nahan — feeds the Pabbar, Shimla.
  • Beas Kund — source of the Beas.
  • Parvati, Sonapani, Gangstang, Perad, Dudhon, Bhadal, Lady of Keylong, Miyar, Pin, Gyephang.
  • Glacier retreat in the Chandra and Spiti basins is a standard environment-paper question, linked to hydel generation and flash-flood risk.
Exam hook Highest peak: Reo Purgyil, 6,816 m, Kinnaur. Largest glacier: Bara Shigri, Lahaul. Highest outer-Himalayan peak: Churdhar, Sirmaur.
